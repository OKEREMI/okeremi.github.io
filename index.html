60-second rounds

Play a Quick Round on the bus, in a queue or on a break. It even works offline and syncs later.

Simple, bright and calm

Big buttons, clear shapes and colours that work for colour-blind players too. Large text and a calm mode are one tap away.

Honest rewards

Rewarded for skill, never for watching ads

Play earns XP, levels and streaks. Adult players can also earn Reward Points from play, streaks and challenges, and turn them into airtime or a bank transfer where this is available.

  • Watching an ad never earns Reward Points. Videos are always optional and only give a small boost.
  • Your points come from a clear record that never changes behind your back.
  • Under-18s play for XP, levels and cosmetics only.
  • Daily limits keep it fair and sustainable for everyone.

Questions

Is Globe Rush free?

Yes. The game is free to download and play. It's supported by optional ads and sponsors.

Do I need to read English to play?

No. Gameplay uses only shapes, colours, digits and simple symbols. Menus can be translated later, but the puzzles never need words.

Can I earn money by watching ads?

No, and that's on purpose. Ads only ever give an optional boost, such as 10 extra seconds. A boosted round earns XP only, never Reward Points or a prize rank.

Where is Globe Rush available?

We're preparing a first test in Nigeria on Android. More countries will follow.

What phones does it work on?

Android 7.0 and newer. It's built to run smoothly on low-cost phones and to use little data.

How do you use my data?

We collect only what the game needs to work and stay fair. Read the privacy policy.
